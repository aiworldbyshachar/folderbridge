# FolderBridge demo film - storylines

Same family film as ClipBridge: ~80-90 s, 1920x1080, the warm desk (MacBook Air left, 27" PC monitor right), George's voice, the accent bridge as the signature visual, the real app UI. One object travels through the whole film. Shape: hook -> the old way (3 detours) -> "what if..." + reveal -> core action both ways -> pairing + the one rule -> depth -> privacy -> six languages -> payoff -> end card.

**Picked (2026-11-04): A, the wedding video.**

## A. The wedding video (recommended, picked)

**Object:** a folder `Wedding - edit` on the Mac with a 2.4 GB video inside. It has to get to the PC, where the editing software is.

- **Hook (0-12 s):** "A Mac... and a PC. The video is here. The editing is there." The folder sits on the Mac's desktop.
- **The old way (12-19 s):** a drawer opened, hunting for a USB stick; an email "Attachment too large (25 MB limit)"; a cloud upload bar at 3%, "about 2 hours left". [sighs]
- **Reveal (19-25 s):** the accent bridge draws between the screens, carrying a folder; the FolderBridge icon blooms; the name wipes in.
- **Core action (25-33 s):** the folder dropped into FolderBridge on the Mac -> on the PC it's arriving, the real progress bar "Syncing 1 file - 1.9 GB left". A cut made on the PC comes back to the Mac.
- **Pairing + the one rule (33-43 s):** the real Settings: one code typed on the other computer, "Connected". "The one rule: same Wi-Fi."
- **Depth (43-58 s):** the activity list (who changed what, when); a file changed on both -> "Keep both"; a deletion -> the trash -> Restore. "Nothing is ever lost."
- **Privacy (58-68 s):** the cloud crossed out, the bridge with a lock: direct, encrypted, no account, no storage limit; Pause in one click.
- **Six languages (68-73 s):** the UI switching language on each spoken name, RTL flips.
- **Payoff (73-79 s):** back on the PC, the video plays in full - "the same folder, on both".
- **End card (79-86 s):** icon + wordmark, "Free & open source · Mac & Windows", "Download free" + URL.

Why: big files are where the old ways hurt most (USB, email limits, slow cloud) and where FolderBridge is plainly better. A video playing at the end is a clear, visual payoff.

## B. The Q3 launch folder

**Object:** a work folder `Q3 launch` (plan, budget, slides) used on both computers - the same "Q3 launch" world as the ClipBridge film, so the two films feel like one family.

- **Hook:** the plan is on the Mac, the budget is on the PC, and they need each other.
- **The old way:** `plan-final-v7.docx` emailed back and forth; "which one is the latest?"; a shared drive asking to sign in.
- **Reveal, core, pairing:** as in A, with small office files that appear instantly.
- **Depth:** the twist moment - the plan edited on both computers - FolderBridge asks, Keep both, both versions side by side. Then renames and folders travel.
- **Payoff:** the meeting starts, both computers open the same, latest plan.

Why: strong link to ClipBridge's film and very relatable for office work; less spectacular than a big video.

## C. The family photos

**Object:** `תמונות - טיול 2026` (Hebrew name), hundreds of photos from the phone, imported on the Mac, needed on the family PC.

- **Hook:** the photos are on the Mac; grandma's PC is where the family looks at them.
- **The old way:** a full USB stick ("not enough space"), a messaging app shrinking the photos, a cloud subscription offer.
- **Core:** the whole folder flows across; Hebrew names arrive exactly right.
- **Depth:** someone deletes a photo by mistake on the PC -> the trash -> Restore -> it's back on both. "Nothing is ever lost."
- **Payoff:** the slideshow plays on the PC.

Why: warm and personal, shows Hebrew names and the trash; harder to show "big file" and the conflict choice.
